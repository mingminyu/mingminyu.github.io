# 常用词讲解（一）

## 1. literally

如果你经常和美国人交谈，你肯定会听到这个单词 `literally`，但 `literally` 到底是什么意思呢？你如果去查一个词典，他就会告诉你 `literally` 的意思是**按字面意思来理解**。`Sometimes we do use it that way`（有时候我们确实这样使用），比如说 `he literally walked ten miles`，那这个就是说 “他**真的**走了十个 miles”，不是夸张的。

<!-- more -->

But that's not the only way we use it。We also say things like 

- `it's literally a million degrees outside.`
- `I'm literally starving.` 
- `This is literally the best day ever.` 

那这些句子显然就不是字面的意思。

比如说，`I'm literally starving`. Of course, you're not actually starving. You're just saying that you're very, very hungry. In spoken English, we often use literally as an intensifier. 也就是说，**它的作用更像是在加强语气，让一句话听起来更夸张、更有情绪、更 drama 了**。

所以那些句子里面的 `literally` 根据语境可以理解成 “**真的、简直、完全**” 怎么怎么样，这就是为什么我们学英语的时候不能只记一个中文意思。Because words change over time and the way that people use them in real life can become part of their new meaning.
