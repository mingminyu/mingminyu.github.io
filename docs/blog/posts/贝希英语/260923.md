## 2. Key grammar: have & has

| 词汇/句型 | have | has |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 肯定句 | I /You /We /They + have | He /She /It + has |
| 否定句 | I /You /We /They ==don't== + have | He /She /It ==doesn't== + has |
| 一般疑问句 | ==Do== you /I /They have ...? | ==Does== he /she /it  have ...? |
| 肯定回答 | Yes, I /you /they do. | Yes, he /she /it does. |
| 否定回答 | No, I /you / they don't | No, he /she /it doesn't |

## 3. be动词的转换练习再复习

## 5. 背诵

### 5.1 The Club

The fly, the crab and the crocodile are friends. This is their club.

Our club is a mess. We need to clean up!

I can use the **broom** to sweep.

I can put away the **blanket** and put up the flag.

I can wipe the **globe** with  a **rag**.

Now out clu is clean!

Look at the clock. It's time to eat!
