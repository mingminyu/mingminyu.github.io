## 1. Can / Cannot

!!! note "语法"

    - **肯定**: I / you / we / they / he / she / it + **can**
    - **否定**: I / you / we / they / he / she / it + **cannot**


<div class="grid cards" markdown>
- **Example 1**:
    - I ==can== read.
    - You ==cannot== jump.
    - We ==can== sing.
    - They ==cannot== eat.
    - He ==can== run.
    - She ==cannot== cook.
    - It ==can== fly.

- **Example 2**:
    - We ==can== skip.
    - I ==cannot== skate.
    - You ==can== dance.
    - He ==cannot== climb.
    - They ==can== drive.
    - She ==cannot== shout. 
    - It ==can== write.

- **Example 3**:
    - A fish ==cannot== talk, but it ==can== swim.
    - A bird ==can== fly, but it ==cannot== jump.
    - A monkey ==cannot== fly, but it ==can== climb trees.
    - A chicken ==can== walk, but it ==cannot== write.
    - A cat ==can== eat, but it ==cannot== read.
    - A dog ==cannot== draw, but it ==can== run.
</div>
