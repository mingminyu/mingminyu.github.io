## 2. Key grammar: like & likes

| 词汇/句型 | Do ... like ...? | Does ... like ...? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 肯定句 | I /You /We /They + like | He /She /It + likes |
| 否定句 | I /You /We /They + don't like | He /She /It + doesn't like |
| 一般疑问句 | Do I /you / they + V 原...?  | Does he /she /it + V 原...? |
| 肯定回答 | Yes, ... do. | Yes, ... does. |
| 否定回答 | No, ... don't | No, ... doesn't |
| **核心规律** | 动词变化和助动词 do 和 does 搭配 |

## 5. 背诵

### 5.1 背诵一

Ricky and Sam are at Millie's bakery. 

- No, the door is locked. Milly has the keys and she's at home now. 
- Oh, no! What can we do? 
- Let's eat all the cakes!

Later... 

- Oh, my stomach. 
- What's that? Is it a cookie?

- Hello, who are you? 
- Hi, I'm Mel. What's your name? 
- My name's Ricky. This is Sam and this is Lulu. Are you a cookie, Mel? 
- Yes, I'm a cookie. 
- Wow, you can talk. 
- Yes, I live in cookie land. There are many cookies there, but we are sad.

### 5.2 Don't touch the food

- Hello, how are you? 
- Fine, thank you.
- Hi, I'm Tom. What's your name? 
- I'm Tom. I don't like carrots.
- I like beans. What's your favorite food? 
- Ew! I don't like beans. I like pizza. 
- I don't like pizza. I like ice cream. 
- I like pasta, eggs and rice. 
- Please don't touch the food.
