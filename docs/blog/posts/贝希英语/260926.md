# 阅读: Pinocchio

The Land of Toys is great, but ...

Oh, no! My ears.

In the Land of Toys, all children become **donkeys** one day.

A man buys Pinocchio the donkeys.

<!-- more -->

Come on, Donkey, work!

No!

Pinocchio is **lazy**. The man is **angry**.

Ahh!

A big fish eats Pinocchio. Gepetto is in the fish too.

Father?

My boy!

Pinocchio and Gepetto swim out of the fish. But Gepetto is very **sick**.

Let's go home, father. I can **take care of** you.

Pinocchio works and helps his father. One day, the Blue Fairy comes.

You're a good boy, Pinocchio. You can be a real boy now.

A **real** boy and not a puppet? Thank you!

You see, Pinocchio? Good boys are **always** happy!
