## 2. Key grammar: has的用法

**肯定句**：He /She /It + has

- E.g. He has a pair of blue shoes.

**否定句**：He /She /It ==doesn't== + have

- E.g. He doesn't have a pink dress.

**一般疑问句**：Does he /she /it have ...?

- E.g. Does she have a blue shirt?
    - Yes, she does.
    - No, she doesn't.
- E.g. Does he have a yellow pencil?
    - Yes, he does.
    - No, he doesn't.
- Does it have a long tail?
    - Yes, it does.
    - No, it doesn't.

## 3. 背诵

### 3.1 Ben and the Boat

Ben is in the tub at home.

He has a goat and a toad in the tub.

Ben, the goat, and the toad are in the soap. Ben thinks the tub is a boat.

He rows in the soap. Mom is glad she has her yellow boat.
